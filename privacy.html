Privacy policy – rclone backup

This app is used only by its owner to copy their own files to their own Google Drive account.

  • No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Google Drive data accessed through this app is only read or written on the owner's behalf, on the owner's own devices.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
